# A scroll row never wraps and never clips; a scroll column shows its scrollbar and the consumer makes it focusable

`.strand-scroll-row` is a single row that scrolls sideways: it never wraps (a filter strip that wraps to three lines pushes the content it filters off screen) and never clips (a filter the reader cannot reach is not a filter), keeps `overflow-y: visible` so a child's focus ring is not cut, contains its own overscroll, and hides its scrollbar because under a 32px chip strip the bar is most of the strip and the content is reachable by drag, wheel and focus. Its children must be `flex: none` (or `strand-flex-none`) or they squeeze to fit and the row never scrolls. `.strand-tabs [role="tablist"]` carries the same behaviour for tabs. `.strand-scroll-col` is the vertical sibling: bounded by `--strand-scroll-col-h`, it keeps its scrollbar visible, because in a list of rows the bar is the primary sign that more exists below the fold, keeps `overflow-x: visible` and contains overscroll. CSS cannot make the region keyboard-reachable; the consumer supplies `tabindex="0"` and an accessible name.

Where: `packages/strand-ui/src/utilities.css`, `packages/strand-ui/src/components/Tabs/Tabs.css`
