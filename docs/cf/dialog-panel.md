# The dialog panel's width, shape and sheet placement are tokens; the sheet is full bleed, partial height, level 4

`.strand-dialog__panel` reads `--strand-dialog-inline-size` (default resolving exactly as the previous `width: 100%; max-width: 560px` pair did), `-radius` and `-edge`, so a consumer wanting a different width or a hairline edge sets a token instead of clearing a cap it cannot see. `--align-start` puts a palette under the reader's gaze. `--align-end` is the sheet (design language 11.6, the resolution of 14.8 on a phone): full bleed with both `margin: 0` and `max-inline-size: none`, top-only radius because the bottom corners are off screen, partial height from `--strand-dialog-block-size` so the content behind stays legible, elevation level 4 because it must read as detached from content that stays visible, and an entrance at `--strand-duration-normal` rather than an arbitrary consumer number. The padding ladder is Card's; `--pad-none` clips to the panel's radius and drops the body's own top pad, which would otherwise be a dead band over whatever the consumer put first.
