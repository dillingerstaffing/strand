# The calendar is a well plate: seven fixed columns, bounded cells, a stated remainder

`.strand-calendar-grid` lays a month out as `repeat(7, minmax(0, 1fr))`, never an auto-fit grid: a week has seven days at every viewport, and `minmax(0, ...)` rather than a bare `1fr` because a bare track floors at its content's min-content width, so one long word in a day would skew the plate and break comparison by position. The gap is the rule between cells, drawn by the container's background, so no cell needs a border. Header and week rows are semantic rows for assistive technology and `display: contents`, so their cells land in the parent's columns. A day is a bounded cell (design language 10.6): a settable minimum (`--strand-calendar-grid-day-size`), no maximum, and a content region that cannot grow it; a well past capacity states a remainder count instead of pushing its neighbours. Adjacent-month days are dimmed to gray-500, the text tier, not gray-400. The focus ring is inset because an outward ring on a cell flush against the plate's edge is clipped by the container. Today is a ring so it composes with the selected fill. Exactly one day carries `tabindex="0"`; the grid is one tab stop.
